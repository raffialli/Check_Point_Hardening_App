# Results workbench

Mode: Operate. Scope: replace results presentation, not scan/evaluation or PDF behavior.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Infrastructure ownership stays visible while finding and reviewing a check; replace nested discovery with navigator, list, and evidence.

OWN-WORLD: Light slate navigation, white work surfaces, dark ink, pink selection/primary action. Distinct red and amber status semantics. Fine separators, six-pixel control corners, compact sans-serif typography.

STORY: Select a domain and management/gateway scope, find a check, inspect its original scoped evidence, and use existing approved actions. Categories retains its original complete content. Export full reports regardless of selection or filters.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact header and scan summary above a 250px navigation rail, flexible check list, and 400px evidence panel. Search/status/severity controls above flat grouped rows. Evidence is always tied to the selected check and target. At smaller widths detail stacks without cutting off data.

FORM: User-selected three-pane concept; no new concept tournament. Reference: exec-c179bcb6-50a4-4fae-b7f2-3bd1addc8d33.png. Synthetic headings and fake logo in the image are not production facts; retain actual product name and actual scan content. Explicit user constraint: text-only app identity, no Check Point logo or lookalike, including favicons and generated report branding. Clearly identify this as an independent third-party tool and retain the non-affiliation disclaimer on login and in the signed-in workspace.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
